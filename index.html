4-Step Engineering Case Study

How the project evolved from standard network reconnaissance to a high-speed executive auditing suite.

01
Step 1: The Challenge

Subnet Timeouts & Blindspots

Sequential port probes take minutes to complete on filtered networks. A dropped packet frequently led to silent scan hang-ups and incomplete network visibility.

02
Step 2: Architecture

Concurrent Socket Pools

Implemented Python's ThreadPoolExecutor to spawn up to 200 non-blocking worker threads. TCP handshakes across full port spaces complete concurrently in under 2 seconds.

03
Step 3: Implementation

Origin Geolocation & OSINT

Integrated origin country and ASN company mapping, TTL OS fingerprinting, IIS Windows Server release deduction, and extracted web contacts.

04
Step 4: Deployment

Binary Packaging & Reports

Compiled via PyInstaller into standalone zero-dependency executables for Linux CLI and Windows. The auditor automatically writes an executive dark-mode HTML report.
